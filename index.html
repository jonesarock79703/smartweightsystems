THE OPERATIONAL PROBLEM

Manual counts miss things.

Clipboards, spreadsheets and weekly walk-arounds leave gaps. Between counts, stock runs out, rush orders cost more, and staff spend hours checking shelves instead of running the business.

Stockouts

Production stops or customers wait while you chase an emergency supplier delivery.

Over-ordering

Buying "just in case" ties up critical operating cash and occupies expensive warehouse shelf space.

Lost time

Counting, tallying, and reordering by hand wastes valuable employee hours every single week.

HOW IT WORKS

Five steps, all automatic.

Continuous intelligence once you set your reorder points. No counts, no calls, no manual work.

IoT Sensing Matrix
01

Items sit on smart weight sensors

Load-cell platforms under each shelf, bin, or pallet measure weight continuously around the clock.

Sub-Gram Precision
02

Weight becomes stock count

The system knows the weight of one unit, so it converts the reading into exactly how many units remain.

Autonomous Thresholds
03

You set the reorder point

Choose the level and order quantity for each item, or let the AI engine suggest them from usage history.

Instant Vendor PO
04

Low stock triggers the order

When weight falls below the reorder point, the system sends a purchase request straight to the assigned vendor.

Dock-to-Bin Delivery
05

Vendor confirms and delivers

The vendor accepts the order, ships, and the buyer sees live status until stock is back on the shelf.

TWO-SIDED VALUE

What each side gets.

Transforming the vendor-buyer relationship into a streamlined, automated, and error-free ecosystem.

For buyers

Purchasing, warehouse and operations teams
  • Live stock levels for every item, visible on any screen.
  • No more shelf checks or stressful end-of-week physical counts.
  • Fewer stockouts and elimination of expensive emergency rush orders.
  • Usage-based orders so less capital sits tied up in excess inventory.
  • Instant alerts when consumption patterns spike or when a delivery is delayed.
  • Auditable history of everything used, ordered, and received.

For vendors

Suppliers, distributors, and replenishment partners
  • Orders arrive instantly the moment stock is low, pre-filled with part, quantity, and delivery bay.
  • Better demand visibility to accurately plan production batches and logistics routes.
  • Fewer panic orders and fewer phone calls back and forth to confirm specs.
  • Steady, predictable reorder cycles from connected client shelves.
  • Zero part-number retyping errors across purchase order generations.
  • Stronger customer relationships built on guaranteed reliable supply.
